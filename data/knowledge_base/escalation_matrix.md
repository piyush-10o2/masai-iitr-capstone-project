# Escalation Matrix

A ticket is escalated to a senior support agent or team lead whenever any one of three conditions is met: the ticket has breached its severity tier's SLA resolution window, the same customer has contacted support three or more times about the same unresolved issue, or the customer has explicitly requested a supervisor. Critical tickets that remain unresolved 2 hours past their SLA deadline are escalated automatically to the on-call operations lead rather than waiting for a human agent to notice. Escalated tickets are reassigned within 30 minutes of being flagged and must include a summary of prior contact attempts so the receiving agent does not ask the customer to repeat information already provided. Escalation is a routing decision, not a resolution guarantee — an escalated ticket still follows its severity tier's SLA from the point of escalation.
