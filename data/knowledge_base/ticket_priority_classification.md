# Ticket Priority Classification

Every support ticket raised by a rider, driver, or internal staff member is classified into one of four severity tiers: Critical, High, Medium, or Low. Critical tickets involve active safety concerns, failed or duplicate payments, or account lockouts that prevent a user from completing an in-progress ride. High-severity tickets affect a single user's ability to use core app functionality, such as booking failures or persistent app crashes, but do not involve an active safety or payment risk. Medium-severity tickets cover functional issues with a workaround available, such as a delayed refund or an incorrect fare estimate. Low-severity tickets are general inquiries, feedback, or cosmetic issues that do not affect the user's ability to use the service. Category (Billing, Technical Issue, Account Access, Product Defect, General Inquiry) and severity are assigned independently, since a ticket in any category can range from Critical to Low depending on its actual impact.
